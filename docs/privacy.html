Chessdoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-05

Chessdoku ("the app") is developed by Low Poly Llamas ("we", "us"). This page explains what data the app collects and why. Contact: hossein0nasseri@gmail.com.

What the app does not collect

Chessdoku has no account system, no login, no chat or user-generated content, and never asks for your name, email address, precise location, or contacts. Puzzle progress is stored only on your device.

What the app does collect

Usage analytics, via Google Firebase Analytics: which levels you play and complete, how long a level takes, whether you request a hint, and general app usage events. This is tied to an anonymous device/app instance identifier, not to your name or account.

Advertising data, via Google AdMob: the app shows ads (a banner, occasional interstitials between levels, and optional rewarded video ads to unlock a hint). AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and general usage signals to select and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, as described in Google's advertising privacy policy.

Third parties

Google provides both services used by this app (Firebase Analytics, AdMob). Google's own privacy policy governs how it handles data on our behalf: policies.google.com/privacy. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone beyond these service providers.

Children's privacy

Chessdoku is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us at the address above and we will address it.

Your choices

Most Android devices let you reset or limit your advertising identifier, and opt out of personalized ads, in system settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). You can also request that we tell you what data we hold or delete it by emailing the address above — in practice this is limited to anonymous analytics identifiers, since we don't collect anything that identifies you personally.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page as the app changes. Material changes will update the "Last updated" date above.
